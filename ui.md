# **Air India website: complete visual design breakdown**

Air India's current visual identity is based on its **2023 global rebrand**, developed in partnership with FutureBrand. It combines Indian heritage with a contemporary, premium international airline aesthetic.

The key design idea is *Global Airline. Indian Heart.*

The identity revolves around five elements:

1. Deep red, aubergine and gold.
2. A bespoke typeface called Air India Sans.
3. The Vista symbol, inspired by an Indian window frame.
4. Chakra-inspired geometric patterns.
5. Premium, warm and confident visual communication.

These are documented elements of Air India's official brand identity, not just visual guesses.&#x20;

1. Use whitespace to establish hierarchy.
2. Keep the navigation compact.
3. Use large headings for major sections.
4. Use photography as a primary visual element.
5. Use red for important interactions.

7. Keep the layout responsive.
8. Avoid overly dense dashboards.

## **My final recommendation for FlyWise**

Don't copy Air India's website pixel-for-pixel. Reproduce its **design language**.

The distinction matters:

- Air India's website is primarily a transactional airline platform.
- FlyWise is a conversational customer-support product.
- FlyWise should inherit the colours, typography, imagery, iconography and premium character while optimising the layout for asking questions and reading answers.

I would use an **Air India-inspired editorial landing page with a dedicated conversational workspace**, rather than making the entire application look like a booking dashboard. That will make FlyWise feel authentic to the airline while remaining purpose-built for RAG-based customer support.
